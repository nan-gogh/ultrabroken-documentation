# Master Sword Zuggling

## Summary
Allows you to zuggle the master sword or decayed master sword by desyncing it.

The Master Sword that Link wields in the Prologue doesn't have any special restrictions on zuggling once you have it out of its original map. See _MsgNotFound_ for strategies on performing that first heist.

_Original method discovered by Zas, optimization by mulberry - 6 November 2023_

_Cull Detachment Zuggle method discovered by mulberry - 11th July 2026_

## Instructions
=== "Method 1 (Mineru culling)"
    1. Obtain a Mineru Culled Object.
    2. Bring the Mineru Culled Object to a location where holding a Mineru Culled Object causes you to blink.
    3. Equip the master sword
    4. Hold the Mineru Culled Object and put your back to a wall.
    5. Use the map rune to pause.
    6. Switch weapons to a droppable weapon
    7. View a memory then drop the weapon
    8. Unpause and drop the Mineru Culled Object.
    9. zuggle the newly equipped weapon.

=== "Method 2 (Like Like)"
    1. FE an object
    2. Feed the item that the object is fed to to a like like and also feed it a time bomb
    3. While the like like is preparing to eat it, pickup the FE'd object and face Link's back to a wall
    4. equip MS/DMS
    5. wait until link culls
    6. pause right when the time bomb in the like like explodes
    7. equip a different weapon
    8. watch a memory
    9. drop the equiped weapon, then unpause
    10. Zuggle the newly equipped weapon.

=== "Method 3 (Cull Detachment)"

    === "Regular Master Sword"
        The page on Cull Detachment Zuggle describes the method to zuggle a Master Sword with this technique.

    === "Decayed Master Sword"
        Using Cull Detachment is the easiest way to zuggle the Decayed Master Sword on version `1.1.2` and up. It will also work on older versions if the above methods aren't working for you.

        Follow [the Cull Detachment Zuggle setup for zuggling the Prologue Master Sword](uid:056), but once you arrive in the Prologue at the start of a new game, don't pick anything up. Stop following that guide and return to these steps.

        In the prologue, try not to pick up the weapon at Link's feet. This risks making the MNF droppable (which you **must not** do), or making the weapon type discovered (which makes a later step more risky).

        If Link overload drops his Master Sword, either carefully pick it up and re-equip it, or reload the prologue autosave so that it connects properly.

        1. Complete the Prologue map normally and proceed to the Room of Awakening
        1. Examine the Decayed Master Sword to pick it up
        1. Fill up the Weapons tab:
            - (`1.1.2`) Pick up repeated copies of the weapon at Link's feet. Once the tab is full, swap to one of them
            - (`1.2.0`+) Pick up one copy of the weapon at Link's feet. Unequip the DMS, pick up the drop zuggled weapon to equip it, then pick up more copies until the weapons tab is full
        1. Pick up shield `S2`, and up to three more copies if you want more pause buffer leniency
        1. Pause, drop the equipped weapon, and swap to the DMS
        1. Unpause and wait for Link to cull
        1. Swap to another weapon and unpause to complete the desync
        1. Pause, drop 4 of your 5 unequipped weapon copies, drop all of your unequipped shields, then drop your equipped weapon and swap to the last remaining copied weapon
        1. Pause buffer
            - You **must** pause before the buffer drops complete being processed
        1. Unequip and re-equip your shield to perform Cull Detachment and Swap Resync
        1. Drop your equipped weapon
        1. Reload your manual save

        The Decayed Master Sword will be zuggled on Link when you load back in.
