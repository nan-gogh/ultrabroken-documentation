## Summary

During Mount Lock, Link will attempt to snap to the position of the Steering Stick as long as there is a dependency chain that links him to it. Having a fuse parent equipped qualifies, but causes the Steering Stick to
constantly move away from Link's attempts to snap to it. This causes flight which can be controlled by doing different actions in the air.

#### Part 1: Steering Stick Cold Fuse
---
notoc: true
---

- Part 1 will cover creating a "Cold Fuse" connection between the steering stick and a piece of equipment Link has equipped. It uses Mineru Fuse Entanglement and Mineru Cold Fuse to accomplish this, as they are very accessible.

##### 1a: Mineru Fuse Entanglement
---
notoc: true
---

Our goal is to fuse a weapon or shield to Mineru, but interrupt the fuse so that we can still pick the equipment up and interact with it. This is called Mineru Fuse Entanglement because it is Fuse Entanglement with Mineru as the "parent".

1. Go to Gerudo Town and head a short ways outside the West Gate. Here, summon Mineru.
2. Drop an *equipped* weapon or shield. It must be visibly equipped in-game, equipping and dropping it in one "menuing" doesn't count.
3. Mount Mineru and target the dropped equipment with her Fuse ability.
4. Press Y and pause the game with +/- right after (0-2 frames, to be exact), then:
	1. if (cue), you paused 0 frames after. Watch 2 memories, then unpause.
	2. if (cue), you paused 1 frame after. Watch 1 memory, then unpause.
	3. if (cue), you paused 2 frames after. Unpause.
5. When you unpause, the equipment you fused should fall to the ground. If it doesn't, you:
	1. watched the wrong number of memories,
	2. paused 3+ frames after, or
	3. didn't have the equipment equipped before dropping it.
6. Pick up the equipment onto Link's back and dismount Mineru.

##### 1b: Mineru Cold Fuse

Our goal is to fuse a Steering Stick to the Mineru FE'd equipment, but interrupt the fuse so that it doesn't actually fuse to the equipment (but still becomes "related" to the equipment).

1. Deploy a Steering Stick and target it with Link's Fuse.
2. Mineru will try to exit the camera frame. Steer her to somewhere she's unwilling to go (eg a wall), and she will recall into an orb and fly to Link. The item will turn invisible on Link's back; it is "culled". When Mineru reappears, so will the equipment.
3. 1-2 frames before the item disappears, press the corresponding fuse button to start fusing the Steering Stick to it. It is much better to be too late (the item is already culled) than too early (it doesn't cull in time):
	1. If you are too late, nothing will happen except that fuse closes. Simply try this step again.
	2. If you are too early however, the Steering Stick will fully fuse to the equipment, and you must completely restart!
	3. If you are on time, Fuse will close and the Steering Stick will warp to behind Link while remaining physical.
4. Mount Mineru again and fuse something else to her back; this "detangles" the equipment you entangled to her. You may now dismiss her.

The equipment will be referred to as the Cold Fuse Parent from now on. Make sure it and the Steering Stick don't get deleted (including being put into Link's pouch unequipped).

#### Part 2: Seal Alignment
---
notoc: true
---

- Part 2 covers positioning a Sand Seal next to the West Gate, so that it can be ridden without exiting town.

##### 2a: the whole thing

1. Speak to the attendant at the West Gate and pay for a Sleigh Seal. Unlike wild or standard seals, they cannot be scared, which will help massively.
2. Fire an arrow at the point where the rope attaches to the sleigh to sever it, then Ultrahand the Sleigh out of the way (you don't need it).
3. Equip a shield (it's fine if this is the Cold Fuse Parent) and start riding the Seal. Don't go too far from the Steering Stick, you need it!
4. Turn the seal straight towards the West Gate and ride into it. You will not crash, so just get it somewhere in the doorway (but the more centered, the better). Dismount and observe the Sand Seal:
	1. If it's clearly in front of the doorway and stays put, continue.
	2. If it dives immediately and resurfaces elsewhere, it wasn't on the sand, so try again.
	3. If it's more than halfway obscured by the wall, you may not be able to ride it from inside, so try again.

#### Part 3: Performing Mount Lock
---
notoc: true
---

- Part 3 covers performing Gerudo Town Mount Lock. This glitch requires no actual timing, only steps. However, it *cannot be repeated* without first warping to un-break the Jail Cutscene, so you must not fail!

##### 3a: The Real Thing

Do this one if you're following the guide for real.

1. If at any point during this part, the Sand Seal waddles away from the door (which it can rarely do after idling for a while), make sure to put it back in place.
2. Position the Steering Stick from Part 1 almost fully over the last visible line in the bricks, like the picture below, aligned with the Sand Seal you rented.
	1. From here, you would be able to ride the Sand Seal if not for the Steering Stick prompt.
	2. From here, you are also still within town, so unequipping Link's shirt will trigger a cutscene that sends you to jail.
3. Your goal is to perform a specific sequence of actions in the pause menu so that Link gets shocked off the stick, and then the cutscene is triggered and immediately cancelled.
4. Summon a Shock Emitter, Activate it, and place it so that mounting the Steering Stick immediately shocks Link off of it. Leave Ultrahand as your active ability, or else this gets a lot harder.
5. Activate your shock immunity and equip the Cold Fuse Parent.
6. Mount the Steering Stick, then in this ***exact*** order:
	1. Pause the game
	2. Deactivate your shock immunity
	3. Watch 1 memory
	4. Remove Link's shirt
	5. Watch 1 memory
 	6. Equip Link's shirt and, if possible, reactivate your shock immunity
 	7. Unpause the game
7. If you followed the steps correctly, Link will be stuck to the Steering Stick after you unpause, and will not go to prison (today).

#### Part 4: Freeing Link
---
notoc: true
---

- Part 4 covers detaching Link from the Steering Stick, regaining collision with the ground, and finally clipping back in-bounds. It is the last difficult part of the guide, but it's not as hard as it sounds, I hope.
- At all times during this part, you must avoid getting too far from the Steering Stick.

##### 4a: Detaching Link

1. Link is only stuck to the stick because of a "dependency" between him and the stick. Having the Cold Fuse Parent equipped means there was already a chain between them, so the game did not bother creating a direct connection.
2. Because the Link<->Steering Stick connection is being routed *through* the Cold Fuse Parent, dropping or deleting it **breaks the chain** and detaches Link from the Steering Stick. *You will not have collision.*
3. To restore collision, you must ride the Sand Seal, then climb and jump off of a wall. Observe the Sand Seal:
	1. If it is rolling around, wait for it to right itself.
	2. If it's napping, that's fine.
 	3. If it waddled away, I'm so sorry. Start over. Or hope blindly, and maybe it will waddle back?
4. Swap off of the Cold Fuse Parent to detach Link from the stick, and *immediately* mash A to ride the Sand Seal.
	1. If the Cold Fuse Parent was a shield, make sure you switch to *another shield* (not to nothing), so that you can ride the Sand Seal.
5. Once you begin riding, turn around and ride into the town wall close to or at the West Gate.

##### 4b: Regaining Collision

1. Your goal is to stop riding the Sand Seal, open the Paraglider, and cling to the one of the nearby walls of the caves beneath town.
2. To begin paragliding, hold forward into the wall, tap Cancel (B/X), and start mashing Jump (X/B) to open your paraglider ASAP.
3. The camera will not clip through the ground immediately, but keep gliding forward. You can pause and unpause the game repeatedly if you need to slow down the action.
4. When the camera catches up to Link, you will see the caves just ahead of and beneath him. They will look strange because you are seeing the back face, but the collision is still there. Paraglide into the *wall* of the cave. If you hit the top, you will get stuck and things will get very difficult.
5. After grabbing the wall, climb to somewhere you can jump backwards and land on something. When you do so, Link will have normal collision with the ground except when shield surfing (It also gets a little weird when climbing, but it's hard to notice).
6. If you miss and fall below the caves, don't panic. Do this to recover:
	1. Fire a Hover Stone Arrow below Link
    2. Paraglide down next to it
	3. Use the Quick Menu or Pause Menu to deploy a second Hover Stone on top of the first
 	4. Grab the side of the Hover Stones to start climbing
	5. Jump backwards off the Hover Stones
 	6. Fire another Hover Stone Arrow below Link
  	7. Land on the new Hover Stone
	8. Stand on one corner and face the opposite corner, then deploy a Rocket
	9. Attach the Rocket upright to the side of the Hover Stone to fly back up to the caves
	10. Proceed to Part 5c.

##### 4c: Getting Back in Bounds

1. Climb up on top of the cave. Your next and final goal is to shield surf through the cave ceiling, then stop surfing before you fall through the cave floor:
	1. Hold ZL, perform a stationary jump, and press A to begin surfing.
	2. Repeatedly mash D-Pad Left to open the shield quick menu, then start holding it when you see the menu pop up.
	3. Unequip your shield.
2. If you fall through the cave floor, don't panic. Do this to recover:
	1. Start paragliding
    2. Unequip your shield if it isn't already gone
    3. Fire a Hover Stone below Link
    4. Land on it
    5. Stand on one corner and face the opposite corner, then deploy a Rocket
    6. Attach the Rocket upright to the side of the Hover Stone to fly back up to the caves
    7. Try again.
3. Once inside the caves, ascend to the surface and return to the Steering Stick.

#### Part 5: Mount Lock Weapon Flight
---
notoc: true
---

- Part 5 covers initiating MLWF, how to control it, and what to avoid during it.

##### 5a: Initiation

1. Equip the Zonaite Sword. If you don't have one, most 1-handed weapons will work, just a little worse. 2-handeds are okay but a little unreliable. Definitely avoid spears.
2. With the weapon sheathed, Fuse the Mount Locked Steering Stick to it. Sheathing the weapon will make the first moments of flight marginally more controllable.
3. You will immediately begin flying, but don't panic. Your first goal is to start flying in *some* direction in order to clear the ground. You are likely in a grounded state and bouncing around like a rubber ball, so just start hitting movement buttons until you enter an airborne state and start going somewhere.
4. Once you get airborne, it will become much more controllable. See below.

##### 5b: General Controls

- Whenever Link Carries the weapon, he will constantly try to snap to the Steering Stick, which in turn displaces the Steering Stick and changes his snap target, and so on. This means two things for us:
	- The relative distance and angle between the Steering Stick and Link determines the speed and direction he moves, and
	- The only way to activate and deactivate flight is to pick up and drop the weapon (besides techniques not covered in this guide).
	- We'll call this distance and angle the "Displacement".
- Different weapons have different base Displacements, and different weapon *types* have different animations, changing what Displacements can be reached.
- Link almost always moves along a pure coordinate axis, with some oscillation. Different Displacements are stable along different axes.
	- Some can be stable along multiple axes, which is undesirable as it complicates flight.
- The Zonaite Sword has a very favorable selection of Displacements that allow for relatively highly controlled flight, albeit not as fast as some.
- The subsections below cover techniques which apply to *all* weapons, while the later sections cover how to fly in the desired direction with a given weapon type.

###### Taking Off Reliably

1. To reliably take off upwards, first Ultrahand the weapon so that the Steering Stick faces upwards. If a downwards takeoff is desired, just face the Steering Stick downwards.
2. We want to pick the weapon up onto Link's back, so perform an action that prevents Link from picking up the weapon into his hand, such as sprinting or drawing your bow.
3. Finally, pick up the weapon. So long as it goes directly to Link's back, the initial facing direction will determine your takeoff axis.

###### Landing Reliably

1. To reliably land, we first need to let Link's hitbox catch up to him: Normally your flight is so fast that Link's hitbox lags severely behind him. Dropping the weapon like this will warp Link back to his hitbox and send the weapon flying.
2. Thus, simply perform an action that moves slowly. Most weapons move slowest when shield surfing upwards or downwards.
3. It's unlikely you'll need to wait longer than the couple dozen seconds it will take to descend to the ground while surfing, but if you have issues it can't hurt to give it longer.
4. Once you think the hitbox is caught up (there's no real indicator, but it's pretty quick), start shield surfing downwards. Some weapons move up when surfing "down" and vice-versa, so just pick the one that actually moves you down.
5. A couple seconds before you reach the ground, press Cancel (B/X) to stop surfing.
6. Shortly after the dismount animation ends, drop the weapon. If all goes well, Link and the weapon will both go almost straight down.
7. If you can, open the paraglider right after dropping the weapon. This will prevent Link from flying off on the off chance he gets given a lot of speed.

###### Paraglider Oscillation

1. Opening the paraglider while not shield surfing will quickly cause extremely fast oscillation.
2. Cancelling the paraglide with another action will lock in Link's new position at whichever of the two potential positions he's currently at.
3. Depending on the weapon's base Displacement (with some randomness), this will sometimes have a directional bias. This bias is almost always the same for a given weapon.
4. Biased Oscillation usually results in nearly unbounded acceleration which can quickly take you outside of the map.

##### 5c: 1-Handed Weapon Controls

###### North-South:

- Jumpslash is the primary North-South action. To perform it, press and release Y while not skydiving or shield surfing.
	- To switch directions, paraglide out of the Jumpslash and perform another Jumpslash right afterwards.
	- Skydive or Paraglide returns to Up-Down movement, sometimes switching direction from what was last active.

###### East-West

- A Jumpslash Ready is the primary East-West Action. To perform it, press and hold Y while not performing any other action.
	- To switch directions, paraglide or skydive out of the Jumpslash Ready, press Cancel (B/X) to end the paraglide/skydive, then perform another Jumpslash Ready right afterwards.
	- Releasing Y pivots into a normal Jumpslash through a medium-sized quarter circle.
	- Skydive or Paraglide returns to Up-Down movement, sometimes switching direction from what was last active.
- Throw Ready also travels East-West, but is usually slower and carries the risk of accidentally throwing the weapon. To perform it, first Shield Surf (ZL+A while falling), then Cancel (B/X) the surf and open a Quick Menu, and finally hold R while the Quick Menu closes.
	- To switch directions, paraglide out of the throw ready, press Cancel (B/X) to end the paraglide, and perform another Throw Ready right afterwards.
 	- Paraglide returns to Up-Down movement, sometimes switching direction from what was last active.
	- ...Don't release R and throw your weapon.

###### Up-Down

- When skydiving, falling, paragliding, or shield surfing, Link will almost always go Up or Down.
	- To switch directions or fix the occasional sideways flight in one of these states, first Shield Surf (ZL+A while falling), then perform a Shield Surf Attack (Y). This is less reliable with weapons that aren't a Zonaite Sword.
	- Alternatively, first begin falling, then enter Bullet Time (ZR), aim Up or Down, and begin skydiving (R).

##### 5d: 2-handed Weapon Controls

###### North-South

- Jumpslash is the primary North-South action. To perform it, press and release Y while not skydiving or shield surfing.
	- To switch directions, paraglide out of the Jumpslash and perform another jumpslash right afterwards.
 	- Skydive or Paraglide returns to Up-Down movement, sometimes switching direction from what was last active.
 
###### East-West

- Jumpslash Ready is the primary East-West action, but it can also go Up-Down. To perform it, press and hold Y while not performing any other action.
	- To switch directions, paraglide or skydive out of the Jumpslash Ready, press Cancel (B/X) to end the paraglide/skydive, then perform another Jumpslash Ready right afterwards.
	- Which direction you will switch to is somewhat random.
	- Releasing Y pivots into a normal Jumpslash through a medium-sized quarter circle.
	- Skydive or Paraglide returns to Up-Down movement, sometimes switching direction from what was last active.
- Throw Ready also travels East-West (iirc), but is usually slower and carries the risk of accidentally throwing the weapon. To perform it, first Shield Surf (ZL+A while falling), then Cancel (B/X) the surf and open a Quick Menu, and finally hold R while the Quick Menu closes.
	- To switch directions, paraglide out of the throw ready, press Cancel (B/X) to end the paraglide, and perform another Throw Ready right afterwards.
 	- Paraglide returns to Up-Down movement, sometimes switching direction from what was last active.
	- ...Don't release R and throw your weapon.

###### Up-Down

- When skydiving, falling, paragliding, or shield surfing, Link will almost always go Up or Down.
	- To switch directions or fix the occasional sideways flight in one of these states, Jumpslash Ready is usually most reliable for 2-handed weapons (see above).
	- Less reliably: first Shield Surf (ZL+A while falling), then perform a Shield Surf Attack (Y). This may be reliable with some 2-handeds, especially shorter ones.
	- Another alternative: first begin falling, then enter Bullet Time (ZR), aim Up or Down, and begin skydiving (R).

##### 5e: Spear Controls

- Pretty much everything goes North-South, but sometimes you can end up going Up-Down for unclear reasons.
- Jump attack is almost stationary, but slides East-West very slowly.
- I'll keep researching this one, but it's not so important to the guide given it seems to be trash.

##### 5f: Flight Hazards

###### Things that end Mount Lock

Partial (stops the position snapping, but keeps the shield surf noclip):

- Mounting anything else, including unorthodox mounts like:
	- Getting eaten by a Like Like or Frox
	- Getting grabbed by Gloom Hands
	- Using A Skyview Tower or the Lookout Landing Telescope
- Deleting the Steering Stick Club (or the Steering Stick alone, which is possible in some ways)

Full (stops all Mount Lock effects)

- Warping
- Loading a save
- Dying
- Closing the game

###### Stuff to watch out for

- The "Airwalls" around the outside of the map can be clipped through, but you cannot change your action (and thus Displacement) while inside them. They're very thick, so you may have to wait a while until you can turn around.
- If a Blood Moon occurs during flight, you will almost definitely softlock. This is because you keep flying during the cutscene, and the game is waiting for Link to stop moving before ending the black screen.
- Drowning or hitting Voidouts, Lava, or the Lost Fog can result in long load times, but has not been observed to softlock.
- Moving sideways too close to -155 vertically can cause Link to pass above and below the depths load height every couple frames, resulting in extreme loads and lag.
- Paraglider Oscillation can also cause extreme loads and lag if held too long, especially in certain places and ESPECIALLY in Biased Oscillation.
